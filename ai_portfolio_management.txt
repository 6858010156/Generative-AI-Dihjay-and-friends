AI Portfolio Management: Educational Overview

Portfolio management involves choosing and monitoring a combination of investments. Common goals include balancing expected return, risk, liquidity, and diversification. AI-based systems may assist by analyzing historical prices, identifying patterns, classifying risk, or suggesting portfolio allocations.

An investment simulator can compare a concentrated portfolio with a diversified one. Diversification can reduce exposure to a single company or asset, although it cannot eliminate market risk. Risk-adjusted performance metrics, such as the Sharpe ratio, compare excess return with volatility. Maximum drawdown measures the largest observed decline from a portfolio peak to a later trough.

Machine-learning systems need reliable input data and realistic evaluation. Market conditions change, and a model that performs well on past data may not generalize to the future. Human oversight, transparency, and attention to privacy are important when AI is used in financial services.
