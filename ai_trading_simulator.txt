AI Trading Simulator: Educational Overview

An AI investment simulator is a software environment for testing investment strategies without risking real money. Users can simulate buying and selling assets with virtual capital, observe portfolio changes, and compare strategies. Backtesting applies a strategy to historical data, while paper trading tests decisions without placing real trades.

A typical simulator includes historical price data, portfolio balances, trade execution rules, transaction costs, and performance metrics. Some systems add machine-learning forecasts or trading agents. Models such as random forests and LSTMs can be used to forecast market indicators, but a forecast is not a guaranteed profitable trade.

Important measures include total return, volatility, maximum drawdown, Sharpe ratio, and portfolio turnover. A robust evaluation avoids look-ahead bias, separates training and testing periods, and accounts for trading costs and market changes. Simulated results can differ substantially from live outcomes.
